PRISM · HOW TO PLAY

Bend the light into every receiver

Each level has one or more lasers and receivers. Place and turn pieces so every receiver gets light of exactly the color it shows, while no dark sensor gets any light at all.

Controls

  • Place. Tap a piece in the tray, then tap an empty tile. You can also drag it straight onto the board.
  • Turn. Tap a placed piece. Mirrors and splitters turn in 22.5° steps, so light can bend by 45° or 90°.
  • Move or take back. Drag a placed piece to another tile, or drop it on the tray.
  • Mouse. Point at a placed piece for buttons to turn it either way, flip a prism, or take it back to the tray.
  • Keyboard. Tab to the board and move with the arrow keys. Enter places the piece selected in the tray or turns the one under the cursor; R and Shift+R turn, F flips a prism, Delete takes a piece back.
  • Not every piece is needed. From level 3 on, the tray also holds decoys.
  • Hint. The bulb button places one piece in its correct spot and locks it, marked with a star. If you haven't moved for 30 seconds, the bulb glows.
  • Levels. Use the arrows to step through levels, or Levels to jump to any of them. Progress is saved in this browser.
  • Endless. At the bottom of the level list, pick Easy, Medium or Hard for new levels made on the spot. Each one has a code, such as M-48213, and a link you can copy, so a friend can play the same level.
  • Another device. Progress is saved in this browser. To continue elsewhere, open Settings (the gear button) and scan the QR code with the other device, or copy the link. There is also a save code you can type or download as a file. Restoring adds to what is already there; it never removes anything.

Install and play offline

  • Offline. After you have opened Prism once, it works without internet, including Endless levels.
  • Install. In Chrome, Edge or on Android, open Settings and press Install app under App. Prism then opens from your home screen or app list like any other app.
  • iPhone and iPad. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Home Screen app keeps its own progress, separate from Safari: copy your save code in Settings first, then restore it in the app.

Reading the light

Direction arrows

Arrows run along each beam in the direction the light travels. Light sent straight back, by a mirror square to the beam, splits into two lanes with arrows pointing opposite ways.

Finishing a level

When every receiver is lit, rings around them fill up. Keep the board as it is for a moment and the level counts as finished. Any change starts the wait again.

Colors add up

Light arriving at the same receiver mixes: red and blue make magenta, red and green make yellow, green and blue make cyan, and all three make white. A receiver lights only for its own color.

Board

Laser and wall

A laser sends a beam in a straight line. Most lasers send white light; some send a single color. Walls and the board's edge stop light.

Receiver

The ring shows the color it needs. Wrong-colored light leaves a small dot of that color in the middle; the right color lights the whole receiver.

Glass door

A wall made of colored glass. It lets only its own color through, so white light leaves it red, green or blue, and other colors stop at it.

Dark sensor new

Must stay dark. If any light reaches it, it flashes and the level can't be finished. Watch out for the second beam from a splitter.

Portal new

Portals come in pairs of the same color. Light entering one leaves the other, keeping its direction and color.

Pieces

Mirror

Reflects light. Each tap turns it 22.5°, which changes the beam's direction by 45°. Square to the beam, it sends the light straight back.

Splitter

Half-silvered glass. It lets the light through and also reflects a copy like a mirror would, so one beam becomes two.

Prism

Splits white light: green goes straight on, red and blue bend 45° to either side. Tap it to flip which side red and blue go.

Filter

Colored glass you can place. It passes only the colors it shows: a yellow filter turns white light yellow and blocks blue.

Fixed piece new

Comes with the level and has bolts in its corners. You can tap it to turn it, but you can't move it or take it away.
